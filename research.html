White matter, from the inside out

My work sits in neuroimaging and computational biology, using diffusion-weighted MRI to study the brain's white matter — the wiring that connects one region to another.

  • DTI and NODDI processing pipelines to extract diffusion metrics from brain scans
  • Statistical modeling in R and Python, run on high-performance computing clusters
  • Longitudinal and cross-sectional analyses linking imaging metrics to outcomes of interest
