  • Trial Match

    React · FastAPI · SQLite · 2026

    Research recruitment runs largely on manual methods: flyers on a bulletin board, word of mouth, and coordinators manually scanning spreadsheets for who fits a study's criteria. Trial Match is a tool that takes a spreadsheet of candidate profiles and a set of eligibility criteria (age range, condition, location, and other preferences), scores and ranks every candidate by fit, and lets a coordinator review them one at a time in a fast, swipeable interface, cutting what's normally a slow manual screening process down to minutes.

  • Sulcal White Matter and Repetitive Head Impacts

    Developed pipeline in Python and Bash.

    With this pipeline, I analyzed longitudinal diffusion MRI (dMRI) sulcal white matter data, detecting changes associated with repetitive head impacts (RHI) over 2 years. Analyzed the impact of APOE on sulcal white matter.

    Abstract accepted for presentation at the Radiological Society of North America (RSNA) Annual Meeting; two manuscripts in progress and one additional abstract in progress.

  • MRI Acquisition Time Reduction

    Python, PyTorch, Machine Learning · 2022–present

    Trained deep neural networks (U-Net CNN and Swin-UNETR) to estimate diffusion tensors from a reduced number of diffusion-weighted images, cutting the necessary diffusion directions from 88 to 8. Reducing acquisition time improves patient comfort, decreases motion artifacts, and increases the number of patients that can be scanned.

    Analysis in progress.
